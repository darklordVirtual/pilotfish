# Negative results

Results that did not go our way are recorded here as measured. Nothing in this
file is rounded in our favour.

The hypothesis under test is narrow: governed link eligibility is expected to pay
for itself in quota-constrained and regulated scenarios, and to lose to greedy
metric selection on plain uptime during ordinary failover. Section 7.3 of the
design specification commits us to publishing that loss.

No comparison runs have been recorded yet.
